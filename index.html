      TOOLS FOR GROWN-UPS

      Practice that builds confidence

      Simple, repeatable spelling activities plus your printable word resources.

      Try the 10-minute routine

      1. Warm up with a few older words.
      2. Say the new word, explain what it means and use it in a sentence.
      3. Notice the tricky letters before hiding the spelling.
      4. Spell from memory, then check each letter.
      5. Review missed words tomorrow and later in the week.

      Study tips align with spaced retrieval and explicit letter-sound study.

      About the clues and audio

      “Origin” uses the booklet's language group. Some words traveled through multiple languages; the group is not a complete etymology. Definitions are simplified study explanations, not official competition definitions. Spelling bee rules vary by organizer.

      Words and sentences play from short recordings, with no download. If one has no recording yet, your device voice reads it. The optional live voice needs a first download of model files, which the browser caches when storage allows. Speech uses no account or API key. For words such as learned and dogged, pronunciation can depend on meaning and should be confirmed with a grown-up.

      Practice history, stars and voice settings stay in this browser. Lists made with My words also stay in this browser. This site is hosted by GitHub, which can see a visitor's IP address. If a grown-up uses "Look up the missing words" in My words, those words and the IP address go to Wiktionary (Wikimedia). If a grown-up turns on the live voice, files load from cdn.jsdelivr.net, esm.sh and huggingface.co, and those services also see the IP address.
